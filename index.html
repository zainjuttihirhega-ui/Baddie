Privacy Should Be Part of Your Research

Many people focus on whether a website looks attractive or works correctly and forget to consider privacy. Before registering on an unfamiliar platform, check what information it requests and why that information might be necessary.

Avoid sharing sensitive information simply because a registration form asks for it. A website should have a clear reason for collecting personal information, and its privacy policy should explain how that data is handled. It is also safer to use a unique password for each online account — if a website experiences a security problem, password reuse can put other accounts at risk.
